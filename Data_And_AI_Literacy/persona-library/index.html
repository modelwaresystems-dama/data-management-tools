The Know Your Value core: Value = Useful Change for someone else. Effort ≠ Value — if nothing changes, no value was created, regardless of work done. Every persona exists to close that Visibility Gap at its level.

Career tracks & progression

Management track: Professional → Practice Manager → Executive
Technical track: Professional → Senior Professional → Thought Leader
Hybrid track: Professional → Practice Manager → Thought Leader
SFIA ladder (essence): L1 Follow · L2 Assist · L3 Apply · L4 Enable · L5 Ensure & Advise · L6 Initiate & Influence · L7 Set Strategy, Inspire, Mobilise

SFIA is role-agnostic — it describes responsibility, not titles — so multiple personas can reach the top levels. Careers branch by mid-level into a management path (lead teams & functions) and a specialist path (reach the top through expertise & influence, e.g. a Principal/Fellow at L6–7 parallel to a people-manager).

Find your fit — assessment to persona

Two self-awareness lenses point toward the personas and the 10-year direction that play to your natural wiring. Pick your dominant Thinking Style and Strengths domain; the overlap is your natural zone.

Pick one from each row to see your natural persona zone and direction lean.
Read this as fit, not level. Thinking Styles and Strengths indicate activity affinity — the kinds of work you are naturally strong or weak at — and a direction lean. They are not a measure of Mastery (learning depth) or of opportunity. CA&I measures those separately: fit tells you where to aim; mastery tells you how far you are from the target.

The Personal Value Scorecard (common to every persona)

MetricWhat it tracks
Value-Add Time %Share of time on direct value-creating work (from a work audit) — kept on an upward trend.
Stakeholder SatisfactionFeedback / short surveys after delivering work.
Impact DeliveredTangible business outcomes — $ saved, revenue/profit impact per quarter.
New CapabilitiesNew skills acquired / certifications earned that enable future value.

Reviewed quarterly like a business scorecard; can mirror a Balanced Scorecard across business impact, stakeholder satisfaction, internal-process efficiency, and learning & growth. It measures both outcomes delivered and the enablers invested in — so activity is never confused with results.
