The architecture you chose

ONTOLOGY
The rules. Your Multi-Faceted Taxonomy, formalised: the facets, the controlled terms, and the relationships/constraints between them (a Role requires capability-domains at mastery levels; evidence justifies a mastery level; a learning asset develops a domain). One governed source of truth.
▼ governs
KNOWLEDGE GRAPH
The instances. Every learner, their assessed capability state (mastery + evidence + uncertainty per domain), the roles they hold, the evidence they have produced — as nodes and edges that reference ontology terms by stable id. This is where the CA&I writes; the taxonomy never bloats with per-person data.
▼ feeds
CA&I ENGINE
The behaviour. Adaptive assessment, the mastery gates, IRT selection, roadmaps and the Article-4 Assurance Record — all operating over the Knowledge Graph, speaking the Ontology's vocabulary.

The ontology rules (plain language)

Persona performs many Roles; a Role is performed by many Personas. (many-to-many)
Role requires a set of Capability Domains, each at a target Mastery level. (role targets)
Operating Model allocates work to Roles.
Evidence (E2 Knowledge · E3 Judgement · E4 Practical · E5 Workplace) justifies a Mastery level for a Learner × Capability Domain. (the gate model)
Learning Asset develops one or more Capability Domains from one Mastery level toward a higher one. (the learning map)
SFIA Skill-at-Level is recorded independently of Mastery. (responsibility ≠ depth)
Every term and edge carries Provenance — source, confidence, version — per your Governance/Evidence-Provenance discipline.
