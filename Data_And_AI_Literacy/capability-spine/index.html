Organisational Ability
Enterprise-and-Professional Concept →
Organizational Concept → Organizational Ability → Business Capability
Business Capability: an organisational ability to achieve a business outcome, independent of the processes, roles or technology that realise it.
The two branches never merge. They link associatively: Knowledge Area — provides knowledge & practices for realization of → Business Capability (many-to-many). Never "realizes" directly — realisation is done by people, process and technology.

The realization chain (your DCAM↔DMBOK pattern)

Business Capability — requires → Capability Outcome / Requirement — is realized through → Practice · Process · Control · Role · Evidence — draws knowledge from → Knowledge Area (in a Framework)

One Business Capability may draw on several Knowledge Areas; one Knowledge Area serves many Business Capabilities. Matches are many-to-many and version-controlled — never class equivalence, even when labels coincide.

Where the CA&I and the Learner plug in

Persona — performs → Role (Operating Model allocates work to Roles)
Role — requires → target Mastery of specified Knowledge Areas / competencies
Learner — holds → Mastery (M0–M5) of a Knowledge Area, justified by Evidence (E2–E5)
People in Roles — form → Capability Realization — realizes → the organisation's Business Capabilities
The assessment boundary
The CA&I assesses the individual side — a Learner's Mastery of Knowledge Areas and literacies, evidenced and required by Role. The organisational side — Business Capability presence and maturity — is the aggregate view, rolled up from the people realising it (per your blueprint's "optional aggregate views"). Learner state lives in the Knowledge Graph, referencing ontology terms by id.

Concept-type discipline (your naming-collision rule)

required on every concept
Every concept carries a Concept Type: Knowledge Area · Business Capability · Business Process · Organizational Function. Metadata Management [Knowledge Area] ≠ Metadata Management [Business Capability] — the label does not decide identity. Never create equivalentTo/sameAs/subclass on a label match; connect colliding terms only through provides knowledge for realization of.
