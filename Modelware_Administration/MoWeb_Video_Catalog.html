YouTube connection sign in with Google to load live stats

Click Sign in with Google and approve read-only access to your channel's analytics — the page gets a temporary access token straight from Google. No client secret or refresh token needed. The token lives only in this browser tab and is used solely for Google's own API. For sign-in to work, the page must be opened from a web address you've added to your Google OAuth client's Authorized JavaScript origins (e.g. your own site or http://localhost) — a bare downloaded file or the in-app preview won't be accepted by Google.

First-time setup: open Google Cloud → APIs & Services → Credentials ↗, click your OAuth 2.0 Client ID, and add your page's web address under Authorized JavaScript origins (then Save and wait a minute for it to take effect).

Advanced — manual token (no-popup fallback)

Only needed if you can't use the Google popup. Paste a short-lived Access token, or supply Client Secret + Refresh Token and the page will exchange them (may be blocked by the browser). Sign-in above is the recommended path — leave these blank otherwise.
