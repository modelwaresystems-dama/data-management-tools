Data Management Maturity

The Data Management Maturity Assessment (DMMA, DMBOK) is the evidence base for governance readiness — the level of data-management maturity across every knowledge area, assessed with the Business Data Stewards in the business departments. Data Governance owns closing the gap by defining, implementing and operationalising the data-management practices across the organisation. Each knowledge area links to the policy domain that codifies its controls, and each steward maps to the Operating Model.
