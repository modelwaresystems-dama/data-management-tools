KSCA Mapping

The drill-down beneath activity affinity. Each Best Practice (DMBOK activity) requires a specific set of Knowledge, Skills, Competencies and Attitudes. This is a team requirement mapped onto the people who do the work: K/S/C are developable and assessed per individual (mastery M0–M5, pooled across the team); Attitudes are dispositional and composed across the team (fit & coverage, not trained in). The CA&I assesses both the individual and the team. Knowledge spans several frameworks (DMBOK, BizBOK, BABOK, PMBOK, Change & Records Management); Skills are SFIA skills; Competencies are the SFIA generic attributes; Attitudes are Gregorc Thinking Styles (with sub-elements) and CliftonStrengths themes, to the theme level (Woo, Learner…).
