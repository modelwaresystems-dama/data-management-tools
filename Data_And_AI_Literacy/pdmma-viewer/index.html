Professional Data Management Maturity Assessment

The PDMMA plots a Learner's mastery of each DMBOK Knowledge Area — Acquired (today), Required (the role) and Desired (aspiration) — on the named mastery scale. The gap from Acquired to Required is the CA&I "distance to required". This is the third assessment leg, after Thinking Styles and CliftonStrengths.
