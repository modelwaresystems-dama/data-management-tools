Pick a node or proposition to see every source and the exact passage that supports it. This is what the pipeline freezes against at G4 Evidence.

Many citations roll up to one work (e.g. the DAMA-DMBOK chapters and glossary). Grouping is a view only — it does not merge or re-point your committed evidence links. Each source keeps its own id and passages.

CSL-JSON imports directly into Zotero (File > Import, or the Zotero MCP later).
