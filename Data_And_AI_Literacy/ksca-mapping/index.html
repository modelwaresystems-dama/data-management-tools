KSCA Mapping

The drill-down beneath activity affinity. Each Best Practice (DMBOK activity) requires a specific set of Knowledge, Skills, Competencies and Attitudes — the KSCAs an employer seeks and the CA&I assesses. Knowledge spans several frameworks (DMBOK, BizBOK, BABOK, Data Science, Records Management); Skills are SFIA skills; Competencies are the SFIA generic attributes; Attitudes are the natural disposition — Gregorc Thinking Styles (with their sub-elements) and the CliftonStrengths themes, to the theme level (Woo, Learner…).
