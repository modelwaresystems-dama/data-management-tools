Understanding the CDMP

A short guide to the Certified Data Management Professional credential — the levels, the exams, who each is for, and the terms used in this survey and its results.

Figures current as of July 2026 · based on DAMA International's published certification material

What the CDMP is

The CDMP (Certified Data Management Professional) is the professional certification of DAMA International, the global association for data management. It assesses knowledge of the DMBOK — the Data Management Body of Knowledge, DAMA's reference guide covering the full field, from governance and modelling to quality, metadata and integration.

Everyone enters through one exam, the Data Management Fundamentals (DMF) exam. Your DMF result, and whether you go on to complete specialist exams, determines your level.

The certification levels

There are three examined levels, plus Fellow as a separate lifetime honour.

Associate
Pass the DMF exam at 60%+
No prior experience required. Confirms foundational, enterprise-wide knowledge across the DMBOK. This is the entry credential and where most holders are.
Practitioner
DMF + any two specialist exams, 70%+ in all three
No assessed prerequisite; 2–10 years of experience is the intended audience. Demonstrates cross-enterprise breadth plus two chosen areas of depth — a fuller practising-professional knowledge credential.
Master
DMF + any two specialist exams, 80%+ in all three
Plus a minimum of 10 years of relevant data experience, confirmed by CV review. Combines higher examination performance with validated experience — a senior-professional signal.
Fellow
Not part of the exam ladder
A separate lifetime honour: by nomination only, requiring at least 10 consecutive years of DAMA membership, at least 10 years of notable contribution, service or leadership, regional endorsements and board approval. Self-nomination is not accepted.
A common misunderstanding: a high DMF score does not, on its own, make you a Practitioner or Master. Scoring 70% or 80% on the DMF still gives you the Associate credential — the higher score only remains eligible to count toward Practitioner or Master once you complete two qualifying specialist exams.

The DMF exam

The Data Management Fundamentals exam is the single foundation exam everyone takes. A score of 60% or above earns the Associate credential; 70%+ and 80%+ matter later because they are the thresholds needed, across all three exams, for Practitioner and Master.

An official DMF practice exam is available. Exams are delivered in English, with 20 extra minutes offered for the English-as-a-second-language version.

The specialist exams

Practitioner and Master each require any two of the seven specialist exams. Candidates choose which two.

  • Data Modeling and Design
  • Metadata
  • Data Quality
  • Data Governance
  • Data Warehousing & Business Intelligence
  • Reference and Master Data Management
  • Data Integration and Interoperability

Each specialist exam draws on the whole DMBOK, not just its named area, so candidates are expected to connect their chosen field to the rest of data management. That cross-domain requirement is what makes Practitioner structurally more than "the DMF three times."

Note: there is currently no official Data Architecture specialist exam. Data Architecture training is valuable professional development, but it is not one of the seven DAMA specialist exams.

Fees, timing and renewal

Each examUSD 311 per online examination.
PractitionerAround USD 933 in first-attempt exam fees (DMF + two specialists).
MasterThe same three exams, plus a USD 50 experience assessment after approval.
Take windowA purchased exam must be taken within 12 months.
Combine windowEach exam result can be assembled toward a higher level for only 3 years from badge issue.
RenewalInitial CDMP certification is valid for 3 years; after that, level credentials are maintained annually.

Fees are set by DAMA/ICCP and may change — treat the amounts above as indicative.

Acronyms used here

CDMP
Certified Data Management Professional — DAMA's certification.
DMF
Data Management Fundamentals — the single foundation exam everyone takes.
DMBOK
Data Management Body of Knowledge — DAMA's reference guide, the basis of the exams.
DAMA
The global Data Management Association; DAMA SA is its South African chapter.
DW/BI
Data Warehousing & Business Intelligence — one of the specialist exams.
CPD
Continuing Professional Development — ongoing learning that maintains a credential.
ESL
English as a Second Language — the exam version with 20 extra minutes.

What the survey segments mean

The analytics group each respondent by their current CDMP status. These are the labels you'll see on the results.

DMF candidate
Preparing for the DMF exam — not yet certified.
Associate
Has passed the DMF exam (60%+). The foundation credential.
Specialist candidate
Preparing for a first specialist exam, or has passed one — on the way toward Practitioner.
Practitioner
Has completed DMF plus two specialist exams at 70%+.
Master
Working toward, or has achieved, DMF plus two specialists at 80%+ with 10 years' experience.
Lapsed
Previously certified, but the certification has lapsed.
