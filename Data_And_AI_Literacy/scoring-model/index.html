Scoring model — corrected (fixes D1, D2, D3)

The reference implementation of the capability-scoring engine, with the three defects from the blueprint fixed and a live test suite that proves it. This is what the DLK pilot's scoring module is built from. Scores are on the 0–5 rubric; enter a channel as a comma list of observations.

What was broken, and the fix

D1 — you could skip judgement to M5 correctness

The original DefensibleMastery gated M5 only on Practical + Workplace and M4 ignored Workplace entirely — so a polished artefact plus a good validation chat could mint M5 over thin reasoning.
Was
M5 = Practical≥.75 AND Workplace≥.75
M4 = Judgement≥.70 AND Practical≥.70
(knowledge/judgement never checked for M5)
Now — cumulative gates
M5 = K≥.60 ∧ J≥.70 ∧ P≥.75 ∧ W≥.75
M4 = K≥.60 ∧ J≥.70 ∧ P≥.70
M3 = K≥.60 ∧ J≥.60
Each band needs every lower channel.
Basis: the blueprint states M5 "requires validated evidence of judgement." Strong practice with weak knowledge is not silently promoted or capped — it is flagged Uncertain — validation required (Stop D), so a human resolves whether the knowledge score mis-measured a genuinely capable person.

D2 — the gates averaged over a critical miss correctness

Every channel used AVERAGEIFS, so one wrong answer on a critical item was washed out by easy correct ones. The narrative demanded "two consistent observations"; the formula never checked consistency.
Was
Knowledge = AVERAGE(all K items)
[1.0, 1.0, 0.0] → mean .67 → qualifies ≥.60
Now — mean + floor + count
channel meets T  ⇔
  mean ≥ T  AND  min ≥ T − tol  AND  n ≥ minObs
[1.0,1.0,0.0]: min 0 < .40 → does NOT meet .60
A disqualifying error hard-caps the capability at M1.

D3 — the next-item priority was unstable efficiency

Multiplying five hand-tuned factors meant any single zero collapsed the item to zero and the ranking swung wildly — and it never actually computed uncertainty reduction.
Was
priority = info × role × risk × gap × fresh − burden
role = 0  →  whole item = 0 (even if risk = high)
Now — weighted additive, bounded
priority = Σ(wᵢ · factorᵢ) / (1 + burden)
info = uncertainty × item-information(difficulty, ability)
mandatory/risk-triggered items override to top.
