Gregorc model · Data & AI Talent Strategy

How you perceive and order information.

Anthony Gregorc's model describes thinking as two choices the mind makes — how it perceives (Concrete, through the senses, or Abstract, through ideas and relationships) and how it orders (Sequential, in a line, or Random, in chunks). Those combine into four styles. You'll mark two words in each of fifteen groups; the result shows where your preferences sit and what that means for how you work, learn and lead.

Scoring runs entirely in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose to export or save your result.
