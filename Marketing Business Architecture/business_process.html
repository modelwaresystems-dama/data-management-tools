Process Step Architecture

Each atomic step and what it is accountable for — execution type, decision, information-concept CRUD/lifecycle, data-product impact, control, record and evidence. Every step carries one primary execution type on the H / A / AIS / AIE / DS taxonomy.

Lifecycle — BPMN 2.0 swimlane

The same steps as a governed flow across three lanes — business operations, AI/automation and human review & approval — with decision gateways and human-in-the-loop points.

Framework alignment. Processes are classified on the APQC Process Classification Framework (category → process group → process → activity → task) and, where they touch banking service domains, mapped to BIAN service domains. This execution-level layer adds the org-specific step grain beneath those reference spines — every step carrying one primary execution type (H / A / AIS / AIE / DS) with an explicit Responsible and Accountable party.

Information Concepts & lifecycle

The business concepts the processes create, read, update and retire — each owned/stewarded by a capability, realised by a data product, and governed through an explicit state lifecycle.

Record & Evidence architecture

Records are evidence that a step occurred, a decision was made, a control operated or an obligation was fulfilled — each with an owner, retention and disposal rule, and a linked evidence pattern.
