Ethical Stewardship

The higher-order layer that sits above Corporate, Data and AI Governance. Where governance asks “can we do this safely and compliantly?”, stewardship asks “should this exist at all, for whom, under whose authority, with what human benefit, and with what long-term consequences?” It runs as an absolute pre-gate — Purpose, Collective Benefit, Authority, Equity and Sustainability must be answered before the Value × Readiness and technical delivery gates.

How the pre-gate sits above the delivery gates

Ethical Stewardship decides should we do this? before Value × Readiness decides should we do this now? and before the technical gates ask can we build and run it responsibly?

Governance model

An Ethical Stewardship Council sits above the existing forums. It does not duplicate them — it makes the go / no-go / redesign decision before AI readiness, data quality, policy-as-code and deployment gates are applied.
