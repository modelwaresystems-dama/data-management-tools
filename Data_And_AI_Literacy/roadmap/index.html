Locked decisions (your picks, 29 Oct 2026)

The three calls that shape everything — and the honest caveats

Foundation Multi-tenant platform (D0) + full custom web app (D1) + real IRT/Bayesian scoring (D2). DLK is tenant #1 and the live pilot. This is the ambitious end of the register — a platform play, not a quick tool.
Your taxonomy is the spine The existing Modelware Taxonomy (resources ↔ students ↔ assessment/development state) becomes the capability-learning map. We map SFIA/DMBOK by reference onto it (D7) rather than inventing a parallel structure.
Caveat 1 — synthetic data for IRT Synthetic examinees cold-start the item parameters so the engine runs before DLK generates volume. They cannot validate real-world item difficulty or differential item functioning — only real responses do. Synthetic is the bootstrap; recalibration on DLK's real responses (Phase 2) is non-negotiable before any high-stakes use.
Caveat 2 — protect the lean scope The lean DLK pilot only stays lean if the multi-tenant + IRT ambition is held behind it. The pilot ships on a rules engine (with D1–D3 fixed) seeded for IRT; IRT activation, extra tenants and professional depth come after DLK validates the model. Scope creep here is the single biggest risk to the 6–8 week window.
Caveat 3 — content cold-start ~37 items exist in full today; the model needs hundreds. Item authoring + rubric engineering is a continuous workstream from week 1, not a one-off. The pilot's item count is deliberately small and capability-targeted.

Phased plan

Phase 0 — Decide & de-riskweeks 1–3 Clear the gates that would otherwise block a clean pilot.›

Do

  • Fix scoring defects D1–D3: gate each mastery band on all lower channels + a consistency/floor check; replace the multiplicative priority with a stable additive form.
  • Confirm SFIA licence + DAMA/DMBOK usage rights for commercial use; agree the "map by reference, don't reproduce descriptors" approach.
  • Draft the DPA (Modelware = processor, DLK = controller of the Assurance Record) and the Article-4 "evidence, not compliance certification" positioning language.
  • Ingest the Modelware Taxonomy as the capability-learning spine; confirm the 20 capability domains map onto it.
  • Stand up the thin multi-tenant skeleton: auth, tenant isolation, learner identity, consent capture — minimum to host DLK safely.
  • Design the response data schema IRT can later be fitted to (item, learner, raw + normalized score, confidence, context, timing, evidence level).
Exit when: defects fixed & unit-tested · licences confirmed or a fallback taxonomy agreed · DPA drafted · tenant skeleton hosts a test learner.
Phase 1 — Lean DLK pilotweeks 4–10 One live tenant, real learners, the smallest system that proves the model.›

Ship

  • Universal baseline (Tier A/B), context/role router, confidence calibration, rules-based adaptive branching (defects fixed), stop rules.
  • LLM-assisted rubric scoring + mandatory human review on M3+ and anything consequential (D4).
  • Private learner dashboard + personal roadmap; manager view limited to targets & aggregate needs (governance controls on).
  • Minimal Article-4 evidence ledger per DLK role/context.
  • Synthetic examinee seeding to cold-start item parameters; capture every real response in IRT-ready shape.
  • Small, capability-targeted item set (grow the bank continuously).

Measure (pilot success criteria)

  • Novices not overwhelmed · experts not forced through basics · self-report discrepancies detected · practical evidence changes conclusions · recommendations explainable & accepted · managers cannot misuse individual results.
Exit when: DLK cohort completes · capability vectors (not a single score) produced · pilot criteria met · real response volume accumulating.
Phase 2 — IRT activation + multimodal evidencemonths 3–5 Turn the bootstrap into a calibrated engine; widen how evidence is gathered.›

Do

  • Fit IRT on synthetic + accumulating DLK responses; recalibrate item difficulty/discrimination on real data; retire weak items.
  • Add scenarios, practical tasks, artefact submission, mentor validation, uncertainty states.
  • Open DMBOK/CDMP and AI-governance branches.
  • Stand up tiered human validation as a paid Modelware service (D5): AI+practical to M3, human for M4+/professional.
Exit when: IRT parameters calibrated on real responses · multimodal evidence flowing · validation service running.
Phase 3 — Learning-ecosystem orchestrationmonths 5–8 Make it a recommendation layer over the whole estate, not another LMS.›

Do

  • Map the Modelware asset estate (webinars, videos, courses, Learning Coach modules, exercises) via the Taxonomy spine at outcome level.
  • Connect delivery channels (Face-to-Face, VITL, DIY); activate the Copilot Learning Coach roles (onboarding, roadmap explainer, low-stakes tutor, evidence intake).
  • Prerequisite-aware next-best-learning recommendations.
  • Instrument improvement metrics (D8): recommendation acceptance → completion → re-demonstrated capability delta.
Exit when: recommendations draw on the real estate · Learning Coach live · improvement metrics reporting.
Phase 4 — Professional / SFIA + multi-tenant scale-outmonths 8–12 Add professional depth and bring on tenant #2.›

Do

  • Versioned SFIA skill-at-level mappings, workplace evidence, professional portfolios, aspiration paths — course completion kept separate from competence claims.
  • Onboard a second tenant; exercise true multi-tenant isolation at load.
  • Run the ring-fenced fairness/DIF study (D9) now that real response volume exists — separately consented, isolated from the learner profile, method published.
Exit when: professional branches live · 2+ tenants in production · fairness study results published.
Phase 5 — Mature lifelong platformyear 2+ The never-ending capability graph, humane and funded.›

Do

  • Event-driven + learner-pulled reassessment with a per-period burden budget and annual consent refresh (D10); subscription funds the lifelong layer.
  • Regulatory/content freshness, role transitions, organisational capability heatmaps.
  • Longitudinal validation and recommendation evaluation (does the system actually improve capability?).
Exit when: it runs itself — no END state, learners move through Assess → Learn → Apply → Evidence → Validate → Grow → Reassess.
